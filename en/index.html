About

Theater director and teacher. Graduate of Anatoly Vasilyev's studio, teaches at GITIS. Stages plays across Russia, leads seminars and educational projects, and pursues theater-studies research.

Biography

I treat theater as a living space rather than a workshop for producing spectacle: here the text, the actor and the audience meet for the first time, and the interest lies in what happens at the moment of that meeting — when meaning has not yet set, when a word is only just becoming an action.

For me, directing is not about instructions and results, but about creating the conditions for a meeting: between a person and a text, between an actor and his own material. At the center of my work is a slow, almost laboratory process with the actor — with his body, voice and inner freedom.

My path was shaped by my apprenticeship with Anatoly Vasilyev. It is less a toolkit of specific devices than a way of holding the stage: rigorous, attentive to detail, and at the same time open to improvisation.

Research

I am working on the legacy of Anatoly Vasilyev's "theater grammar" — trying to understand how the movement from text to stage is organized: what decisions an actor makes, how a stage situation is born, and what in this process can be articulated and what remains unutterable.

Reports, articles and working materials will appear in the section "Theater Studies".
