Pedagogy

I build my practice as an unbroken path from K. S. Stanislavsky to M. O. Knebel, and from her on to Anatoly Vasilyev, in whose studio I studied. This also shapes my approach to working with students: attention to "theatre grammar", to craft and to a living dialogue with the text.
